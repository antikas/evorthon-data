# Engagement lifecycle

The runtime `evorthon_data.engagement` aggregate owns the versioned lifecycle and named human decisions. Koine presents its readable records; it does not hold a second lifecycle state machine.

An engagement moves through setup, framing, mode-specific discovery, design, an approved delivery contract, delivery evidence, verification-result identities and named human acceptance. Existing-estate discovery is available only to modernisation; greenfield discovery records required capabilities and constraints without inventing an estate.
