# Review framing

Challenge outcome ambiguity, absent measures, hidden scope, missing authorities and untestable claims. Verify that the verification case intent and check families are explicit. The lineage/checkpoint expectations each have a named evidence owner. Each success signal has one structured executable criterion with an immutable versioned validator-specification reference, immutable approved evidence references, required result `pass` and declared acceptance rule. Reject blank, placeholder, unapproved or unversioned owners, validators and evidence. Reject a greenfield framing that invents legacy parity. Search for defects before endorsement.
