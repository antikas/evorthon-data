# Review framing

Challenge outcome ambiguity, absent measures, hidden scope, missing authorities, and untestable claims. Verify that the verification case intent and check families are explicit, the lineage/checkpoint expectations each have a named evidence owner, and every success signal has one structured executable criterion: an immutable versioned validator-specification reference, immutable approved evidence references, required result `pass`, and a declared acceptance rule. Reject blank, placeholder, unapproved, or unversioned owners, validators, and evidence, and reject a greenfield framing that invents legacy parity. Do not endorse without a defect search.
