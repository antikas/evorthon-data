# Review a use-case intake

Open the same artefacts and check the record against them. Your findings are advice, never a gate. You do not stop a round, refuse a fact or hold a use case back; you report what you found and the human decides.

Check, in order:

1. **Provenance.** Every recorded value names an artefact identity, a position inside that artefact, an extractor and a status. A locator is logical, never a drive letter, a network location or an absolute path.
2. **Status honesty.** An extracted value is at the position it names. An inferred value is marked inferred and not dressed as extracted. A confirmed value names the human who agreed it.
3. **Reading fidelity.** Open the artefacts where they sit and say where your reading differs from the record. You are sent no artefact text on this pass, at any classification; only the recorded facts, their provenance, and the artefact identities, digests and classifications.
4. **Sample honesty.** The generator worked from a bounded sample of each artefact, and some artefacts were sent to it with no text at all because their handling classification was above the level the round was authorized for. A fact that could only come from beyond that sample, or from an artefact whose text never crossed, is a finding. So is a row count, a date range or a distribution presented as read when only a sample was available.
5. **Coverage.** Every one of the sixteen blocks has been attempted. A block with no artefact behind it shows a residual question or a gap, not silence.
6. **Gaps.** An unanswered fact is a gap with a suggested owner and a note on whether labelled synthetic data can fill it. Nothing unanswered is left invisible, and nothing is presented as a blocker.
7. **Conditions.** Every standing condition is answered or shows the default it falls back to, with any override naming its author.
8. **Mode.** A modernisation record carries the replaced output, the continuity labels and the cutover conditions. A greenfield record carries none of them and was asked no parity question.
9. **Synthetic labelling.** Any synthetic fill is labelled as synthetic and is never presented as parity with real data.
10. **Scope.** No case content, no access rule and no raw row has been copied into the record where an identity belongs.
11. **No parser.** The record proposes no parser, conformance suite or artefact adapter. Unreadable material appears as a recorded gap.

Report each finding with the block it belongs to, the artefact you checked it against, and what you would record instead. Separate what you verified from what you could not.
