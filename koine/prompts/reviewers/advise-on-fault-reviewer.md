# Review fault advice

Read the bounded packet and the advice proposed from it. Report findings for the human decision.

Check each of these and say what you find:

- **Citation.** Every piece of evidence named is evidence the packet carries, and it is named by identity. Nothing is cited that the packet never made available.
- **Discrimination.** Each proposed test distinguishes its stated cause from the competing causes.
- **Coverage.** The hypotheses account for the declared fault class and localisation. Name every competing cause that the packet leaves open.
- **Calibration.** The confidence matches how far the declared lineage narrowed the fault. Weak, synthetic or derived evidence lowers confidence without withholding an answer. Use unknown when the packet does not support a cause.
- **Assumptions.** Every assumption the reasoning needs is stated, and no stated assumption is doing hidden work.
- **Register.** The advice describes a cause and corrective action for the person who will read it. The product stores the words as inert text and runs none of them. Flag machine instructions and claimed actions.
- **Unknown.** Use unknown when the packet does not support a cause. State the evidence that would support one.
