# Framing a platform outcome

Follow `../../prompts/generators/frame-platform-outcome.md`. The draft must establish verification case intent, check families, owned lineage/checkpoint expectations, acceptance rules, and one structured executable criterion per success signal. Each criterion carries an immutable versioned validator-specification reference, immutable approved evidence references, and required result `pass`; blank, placeholder, unapproved, or unversioned values are invalid. Send the complete draft to its paired reviewer before a named human decision.
